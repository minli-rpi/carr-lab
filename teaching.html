Courses at Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute

Graduate

CIVL 6960 – Structural Reliability

Spring 2024, Fall 2025

This course covers fundamental concepts and methods in structural reliability, including probability theory, random variables, reliability index, Monte Carlo simulation, first- and second-order reliability methods (FORM/SORM), and system reliability.

Graduate

CIVL 6390 – Wind Engineering

Fall 2024, Fall 2026

This course develops an understanding of, and integrates skills across, the fields of fluid mechanics, meteorology, climatology, bluff-body aerodynamics, structural dynamics, code provisions for design, wind tunnel testing and damage documentation.

Undergraduate

ENGR 1100 – Introduction to Engineering Analysis

Spring 2025, Spring 2026

This course provides an integrated treatment of Vector Mechanics (Statics) and Linear Algebra. It also emphasizes matrix methods for solving engineering problems. Students will be expected to learn key principles of Statics and Linear Algebra and to demonstrate skills with vector and matrix manipulations.
